# Record a vaccination and share care details

Nurses and other staff with approved vaccination-recording access use the existing clinic visit workflow. These are staff-entered records; CVSRS does not generate medication doses, diagnose symptoms or recommend treatments.

1. Find the child, confirm the child's identity and verified guardian relationship, then check in and start the visit. Vaccines must have usable, in-date clinic stock.
2. Open the child's clinic record → Give a vaccine. Choose the actual vaccine/lot and stock location; record the actual time, route/site, assessment and consent references as required by clinic policy.
3. Under Care instructions for the family, complete applicable fields: medicine given/advised (name, dose, frequency and duration where applicable), side effects discussed, reactions observed or reported, home care, warning signs and follow-up instructions. Each field accepts 500 characters. Record “none” explicitly where that is the clinical fact; a blank field is not presented as a claim that nothing occurred.
4. Review before signing shows the vaccine and all entered care fields. Sign and record saves the dose, stock issue and care summary together. A failed command saves none of them. Retrying the same request does not duplicate a vaccination, stock issue or care summary.
5. A verified parent opens My Children → the child → Notes from the clinic. The summary shows the vaccine, staff-entered care information, author/role, clinic/date and a Related vaccination link. The approved vaccination also appears in Vaccination history. Refresh the record to load newly saved information.

BHWs use Staff notes → Follow-up note for observations, family-reported symptoms or instructions relayed from clinical staff. Show this note to the family is selected by default; private operational notes can remain private. BHW membership alone does not grant permission to sign vaccinations or issue clinical medication advice.

Care summaries and notes preserve authorship and remain append-only. Add a further note to correct or update advice; vaccine fact corrections continue through the existing governed correction process. If an approved wrong-child correction moves a vaccination, its care summary follows it to the correct child and family; unrelated follow-up notes stay with their original child. Existing guardian verification, clinic isolation and role permissions remain enforced. No clinical advice is prefilled by the application.

New normal-administration requests accept optional `care` fields. Existing clients without care remain compatible. Already-given/exception records can use the existing event-linked Staff notes workflow; the exception-review and stock rules are unchanged.
